About Aarhasi

A new-age distribution company, built for how B2B actually works.

Founded in May 2023, Aarhasi Global set out to redefine rapid, responsible and relationship-driven distribution in India's premium technology segment — and built it fast.

Aarhasi Global Pvt Ltd was founded by Vikramsinh H. Jadeja and Kiranba V. Jadeja as a B2B, wholesale and institutional distributor of smartphones and consumer electronics across India and abroad. As an Apple Authorized DPP Partner, Aarhasi is the trusted B2B and institutional sales representative for Apple products across Gujarat, with MacBook and iPad as core strengths.

Beyond Apple, the company is steadily extending the same distribution discipline into consumer durables, home appliances and lifestyle products — building toward a broader B2B technology distribution platform rather than a single-brand reseller relationship.

The business is led by a team with 63+ years of combined leadership experience across West, East and South India, drawn from backgrounds at Redington India, Ingram Micro, LG Electronics and OPPO's Gujarat distribution network.

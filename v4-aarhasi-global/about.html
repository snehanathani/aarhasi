About Aarhasi Global

Redefining B2B Technology Distribution in India.

Founded in May 2023, Aarhasi Global set out to build rapid, responsible, and relationship-driven distribution in India's premium technology market — backed by six decades of combined industry experience.

Built for Scale, Channel Trust, and Velocity

Aarhasi Global Pvt Ltd was established in Ahmedabad by Vikramsinh H. Jadeja and Kiranba V. Jadeja as a specialized B2B, wholesale, and institutional technology distributor.

As an Apple Authorized DPP Partner, Aarhasi represents Apple’s institutional and B2B commercial distribution footprint across Gujarat, establishing particular leadership in high-volume MacBook and iPad allocations.

Beyond mobility, the company is actively deploying the same distribution discipline into consumer durables and appliances — creating an expansive, multi-category distribution engine.

"Our model is simple: deliver OEM-channel integrity, guaranteed periodic volume, and unconditional accountability across every tier of the distribution chain."

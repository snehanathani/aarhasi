About Aarhasi Group

An international trading powerhouse, built on a dual-entity advantage.

Aarhasi Group is an international consumer electronics trading and distribution business operating across primary commerce hubs in Asia and the Middle East — serving wholesale partners, retail networks and cross-border buyers worldwide.

India Unit

Aarhasi Global

Established baseline

Focuses on national distribution excellence across India. Built a ₹320 Cr Apple distribution operation and an extensive retail partner ecosystem.

Dubai, UAE Unit

Aarhasi World Trading FZCO

Est. 2025 · Dubai Free Zone

Serves as the international trade engine for global sourcing, multi-currency trade financing, rapid re-exports and cross-border supply chain operations.

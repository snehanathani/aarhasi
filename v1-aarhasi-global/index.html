About Aarhasi Global

A distribution business built on the authorized channel.

Aarhasi Global is a B2B, wholesale and institutional distributor of smartphones and consumer electronics across India. As an Apple Authorized DPP Partner, we are a trusted B2B and institutional sales representative for Apple products across Gujarat, with MacBook and iPad as core strengths.

Beyond Apple, we are extending the same distribution discipline into a wider consumer technology portfolio — computing, imaging and accessories — serving partners who need genuine product, reliable supply and terms that work at volume.

  • Authorized-channel sourcing — genuine product, full warranty
  • End-to-end logistics: packing, warehousing and last-mile delivery
  • Dual-entity reach across India and the UAE
